## 安静，不等于没有设计

当一个界面足够安静时，用户会更容易感知结构。  
这并不意味着它缺少设计，而是说明设计已经退到更深的一层。

## 真正重要的三件事

- 主次关系足够明确
- 留白不是浪费
- 文本像内容，不像装饰

<!--lang:en-->
## Calm Does Not Mean Undesigned

When an interface becomes calm enough, structure becomes easier to perceive.  
That does not mean design is absent. It means design has moved deeper into the system.

## Three Things That Matter

- Hierarchy is obvious
- White space is not waste
- Text reads like content, not decoration
