## 功能可以运行，产品需要边界

一个功能成立，意味着它可以被触发。  
一个产品成立，意味着它知道自己何时开始、何时停止、由谁确认、如何被解释。

## 产品形态的三个信号

### 输入被约束

系统知道什么该进入，什么不该进入。

### 输出可被消费

输出不是一段孤立文本，而是能进入下一个动作的结构化结果。

### 人机关系被定义

用户不是在“看 AI 表演”，而是在一个更清楚的流程里做判断。

<!--lang:en-->
## Features Can Run. Products Need Boundaries.

A feature exists when it can be triggered.  
A product exists when it knows when it starts, when it stops, who confirms it, and how it is explained.

## Three Signals of Product Shape

### Inputs Are Constrained

The system knows what should enter and what should not.

### Output Can Be Consumed

The output is not isolated text. It is a structured result that can move into the next action.

### Human-Machine Roles Are Defined

The user is not watching an AI performance. They are making decisions inside a clearer process.
